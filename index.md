# Countercheck privacy policy

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

Countercheck is an app that helps shop owners notice when someone has stuck their own payment QR code over the real one. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: **Countercheck collects nothing. Everything stays on your phone.**

## What the app stores, and where

When you use Countercheck, it saves on your phone:

- the QR codes you register as genuine, and the names you give the places they're displayed ("spots");
- a history of your checks: when you checked, the result, and the content of the QR code that was scanned;
- your settings, such as the theme and reminder time.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is **not** sent to us or anyone else. We have no server and no accounts, and we can't see your data.

Countercheck turns off Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its data, so it isn't copied to Google Drive or another phone either.

## No internet access

The Countercheck app on Google Play does not have Android's internet permission. That means it **cannot** send anything over the internet, even if it tried. It has no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting.

## Camera

Countercheck uses the camera only to read QR codes while you're scanning. Camera images are processed on your phone and never saved or sent anywhere. The app asks for camera permission the first time you scan.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Countercheck shows a notification at the time you choose. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn reminders on.

## When information leaves your phone

Only when **you** choose to share it:

- **Export backup**, **Export CSV** and **Share incident report** create a file and open Android's share menu. You decide where it goes (for example email, a messaging app or your own cloud storage). What happens to it then is up to that app and its privacy policy.
- **Import backup** reads a file you pick with Android's file picker.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Delete all data** removes all spots, history and settings from the app.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

## Children

Countercheck is a tool for businesses and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.
